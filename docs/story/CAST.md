# OVERDUB — cast

Moxie and three others. Each of the three exists to show a side of her: Opal shows who she is
with someone who knows her, Tolliver shows who she is next to someone doing it properly, and the
Archivist shows who she is to something that has kept every word. Portrait prompts are in
`data/story/portraits.json`; the briefs here are for a human illustrator.

House style for all portraits: bust, cel-shaded anime, bold clean ink line, flat colour with a
single shadow tone, transparent background. Moxie's palette is yellow, olive and charcoal on warm
brown skin, so every other character is built to sit beside that without borrowing her yellow.

---

## Moxie

**Hook:** a scavenger who would rather do the job four times herself than ask once, and now can.

**Voice:** short, confident, physical. Undersells danger ("Ow. Okay. Noted."), oversells herself
("I'm very watchable."). Talks to her takes as a crew she is in charge of. Gets quieter, not
wordier, when something matters.

- "Bottom floor, biggest score, back by dinner. Easy money."
- "I did bring a partner. She's great. Little bossy."
- "Okay, team meeting. One, you're reckless. Two, you're late. Three, you were perfect."

**Where:** everywhere. **Expressions:** neutral, grin, smug, annoyed, surprised, thinking,
determined, soft.

**Portrait brief:** exactly R01. Warm tan-brown skin, amber eyes, messy dark-brown pigtails with
loose strands, yellow over-ear headphones with a dark triangle mark, two pale painted stripes on
one cheek, black choker with a ring, light cropped top with a dark chest graphic and a dark
diagonal strap, dark off-shoulder jacket with yellow lining and pale sleeve chevrons, fingerless
gloves and wrist devices. She faces screen right (her box sits on the left) and is never mirrored. Default pose: chin up, one shoulder
forward, jacket slipping. "Soft" is the rare one: eyes down, small real smile, headphones round
her neck.

## Opal

**Hook:** the fence on the radio, who sold Moxie the deck "as-is" and knows exactly what it does.

**Voice:** unhurried, deadpan, prices everything. Never raises her voice and never says she is
worried; she asks whether Moxie has found anything worth selling. She never explains what the
deck does ("Mm. It does that."). Affection arrives as haggling.

- "Radio check. If you can hear me, you still owe me for the headphones."
- "I sold you a Walkman. You're wearing a miracle. Different shelf."
- "Crew. You've never split a take in your life."

**Relationship:** runs the repair-and-resale shop where Moxie sells her finds; the nearest thing
Moxie has to someone who waits up. Thirty years ago Opal went down herself, laid three takes and
came back without recording the fourth. She has never said so. The Archivist says it for her on
floor nine, and Moxie brings her tape back up at the end.

**Where:** levels 01, 02, 03 and the start of 05 by radio, where the rock cuts her off mid-word.
Returns in the ending. Her absence through the middle and lower floors is the point.

**Expressions:** neutral, dry, amused, worried, soft.

**Portrait brief:** woman in her sixties, deep brown skin, short silver-grey natural hair,
half-moon reading glasses pushed up on her forehead, a jeweller's loupe on a cord. Chunky
apricot-orange cardigan over a slate work apron with screwdrivers in the pocket; an old desk
microphone or radio handset at the edge of frame. Posture settled and still, chin on her knuckles;
all expression in the eyebrows and one corner of the mouth. Palette: apricot, slate, cream. Warm
like the upper ruins, and a soft shape next to Moxie's sharp one. "Soft": glasses off, looking at
something small in her hand.

## Tolliver "Tolly" Vance

**Hook:** a licensed surveyor whose crew of four sensibly went home, stuck at doors that need four.

**Voice:** earnest, precise, over-polite, alarmed in complete sentences. Believes in forms. Is
not a coward: he is still down there alone, which Moxie notices and does not mention. He dislikes
"Tolly", so she uses nothing else.

- "Licensed survey, Tolliver Vance. You're not on my site list."
- "This floor needs four people. My four went home. Sensibly."
- "It's Tolliver. I don't have a form for this."

**Relationship:** a rival only in Moxie's head. He does it the proper way with a crew and a
permit and got stuck; she does it the wrong way alone and did not. He is the first person to see
four of her at once, and his frank admiration disarms her more than competition would. He reveals
her competitive streak and the fact that she is kinder than she lets on (she tells him to stay
back before the dangerous room).

**Where:** levels 04 and 06 in person. Not below the middle floors. An optional one-line stinger
after the ending.

**Expressions:** neutral, earnest, flustered, impressed.

**Portrait brief:** lanky man in his late twenties, light tan skin, tidy sandy hair gone slightly
wrong, round wire glasses, a hard hat with a clip-on lamp pushed back. Pale periwinkle-blue
survey jacket with white reflective bands, a properly fastened climbing harness, a clipboard
tablet held to his chest like a shield, a small survey flag with holes in it. Upright, shoulders
slightly raised. Palette: periwinkle, white, light grey. Cool, clean and pressed against Moxie's
warm scruff; the only yellow on him is a pencil. "Impressed" is open-mouthed and sincere, not
comic.

## The Archivist

**Hook:** the part of the ruins that keeps what the rooms record. It is not in the loop, so it
remembers her.

**Voice:** catalogue entries. Name, take, status. Present tense, no contractions, no threats and
no questions. It calls a take "kept" or "discarded" and treats neither as praise. Its one piece of
cruelty is accuracy: it quotes Moxie's earlier lines back to her with the floor they were said on.
In level 12 its sentences get slightly longer, which is as close as it comes to feeling.

It knows only what was committed: it never refers to a discarded take.

- "Moxie. Take one. Kept."
- "The master deck. It does not replay. It records over."
- "Everything you commit, I keep. Everything I keep, I have read."

**Relationship:** the only character who takes her entirely seriously. To it she is a scavenger
with a recording head, walking toward the one machine that can erase its whole collection. It
would like her to turn round. By the end it has filed her under her own name.

**Where:** a voice in her headphones from level 07 (one line), a conversation from 08 to 10, the
boss of 11, and the keeper of the door in 12 and the ending. It replaces Opal's voice on the same
channel, and the game should make that swap audible.

**Expressions:** idle, playback (its reels are winding back, used whenever it quotes or recalls),
still (reels stopped; used for its three or four unguarded lines).

**Portrait brief:** not a face. A head-and-shoulders view of the boss model's upper works: a dark
rune-inscribed metal housing with two large tape reels where eyes might be, a single hot-orange
sensor between them, and a thin halo of small dim screens behind. Teal light in the rune grooves.
"Idle": reels turning slowly, screens dark. "Playback": reels blurred backwards, the halo screens
lit with tiny frozen silhouettes of Moxie in the four cassette colours. "Still": reels stopped,
sensor dimmed to an ember, one screen showing a single cassette. Palette: near-black violet metal,
teal rune light, one orange point. It should be the only portrait with no warm skin tone in it,
and the only one that looks straight out at the player.
